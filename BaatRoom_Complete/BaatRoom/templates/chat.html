    KNOW THE ROOM

    Public. Temporary.
    Still worth being careful.

    Everyone can read and copy your messages. This is one public group. A nickname does not verify anyone’s identity. Please keep personal details out of the conversation.

    Ordinary history stays in server memory: at most 100 recent messages for up to one hour. Messages also expire from connected browsers. Restarting the server clears ordinary history.

    The server processes connection information to run the chat and limit spam. Hosting providers may retain technical logs. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; use HTTPS when sharing publicly.

    Reports keep a copy of the reported message, reason, temporary guest reference and time in a separate report inbox for up to 24 hours. The owner can review reports, remove messages, and temporarily mute or disconnect guest sessions.

    Blocking hides a guest’s messages only for you in this browser tab. Temporary restrictions can be bypassed with a new session. Deletion cannot remove screenshots or copies someone has already made.

    No accounts, advertising trackers, analytics, fingerprinting, external fonts or device permissions. A session cookie keeps your temporary guest identity. Theme choices use local storage; local blocks use session storage.
